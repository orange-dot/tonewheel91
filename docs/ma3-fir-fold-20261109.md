# Folded halfband decimators, 2026-11-09

**Decision.** The operator asked for the follow-up named in
`docs/ma3-fma-20261109.md` ("Not done"): symmetric pair folding in the
halfband FIRs. This is step K6, on top of the explicit-FMA commit `d5a127c`.

## Change

The 31-tap oscillator halfband is symmetric, h[t] = h[30 − t]. Its odd taps
are zero, except the centre tap 15 (0.5).

**`decimate_oversample` / `ml_decimate31`** now compute

    out = 0
    for t in 2, 4, ..., 14:   out = fma(h[t], x[t] + x[30 - t], out)
    out = fma(h[15], x[15], out)

- `x[t]` is `history[position − 1 − t]` (mod 31), as before.
- Pairs are taken smallest coefficient first.
- Cost: 7 independent pair adds and 8 fused steps, against 15 fused steps in
  K5.

**`decimate_filter` / `ml_decimate7`** (the 7-tap output halfband) do the
same:
- pairs 0/6 and 2/4 first, then the centre tap 3;
- 2 adds and 3 fused steps, against 5.

**Tables.** `OSC_HALFBAND_31_PAIR` / `_CENTRE` and `FILTER_HALFBAND_7_PAIR`
/ `_CENTRE` replace the nonzero-tap lists. The coefficient tables are
unchanged.

**Twins.** Scalar and lanes do the same operations in the same order, so
they stay bit-identical:
- `test_ma_pool` engine twin;
- the BCS, front and raster lane twins;
- the Glas twin;
- 24 of 24 pairs in the PCM matrix.

**Precondition.** Folding adds two history samples before scaling, so
|x| must be at most FLT_MAX/2 to rule out overflow. The oscillator clamps
its samples to ±1.25, and the 4x decimator's output is bounded by
Σ|h|·1.25.

## Accuracy

`test/ma_fir.c` now checks the folded 31-tap form against the exact
31-tap sum in double precision (each float product is exact in double):
- The table must be symmetric, with zero odd taps outside the centre.
- At every circular position, |error| ≤ 10·2⁻²⁴·Σ|h·x| + 10·FLT_TRUE_MIN.
  That is one rounding per pair sum and one per fused step.
- The history must stay unchanged.
- Inputs:
  - fixed patterns, including subnormals and ±FLT_MAX/2;
  - 2,048 random histories over the whole finite range below 2¹²⁷;
  - 2,048 random histories in the audio range.
- Result: 141,732 results; the worst is 0.373 of the bound.

Two million random audio-range histories (±1.25), error against the exact
sum (`build/ma3-fma-20261109/fir-accuracy.txt`):

| Form | error/signal | max \|error\| |
|---|---:|---:|
| A: 31 taps, multiply and add | −142.5 dB | 3.46·10⁻⁷ |
| K5: 15 taps, fused | −142.8 dB | 4.07·10⁻⁷ |
| K6: folded, fused | −148.3 dB | 1.49·10⁻⁷ |

Folding is the most accurate of the three. It rounds fewer times, and each
pair sum is exact whenever its two samples are within a factor of two of
each other.

**Against K5, same matrix as the FMA note.** The PCM differs from K5 by
−97 to −139 dB relative to the signal. Against the pre-FMA tag the worst
case stays −83.0 dB (Lead, BCS on, Glas off: the BCS phase drift described
in `ma3-fma-20261109.md`).

## Speed

**Protocol.** Run `build/ma3-fma-20261109/runs-fold/` (`run-ab2.sh`,
`ab_table2.py`, `ab-fold-summary.md`, `runs-fold-binary-sha256.txt`).
- Same protocol as the FMA A/B: ten cases, 8 slots, 128-frame blocks,
  256 + 2,048 blocks, PMU, CPU 2, both engines.
- Binaries A, K5 and K6; three epochs, with the order reversed in the
  second; 12:01–12:17.

**Host.** `packagekitd` was active, at about 6 % on another CPU.
- One scalar case, Lead with BCS on, has an outlier epoch: spread 52 %.
- Every other case stays within 3.6 %.
- The table uses medians.

**Reading.**
- **Lanes.** The gain is within noise. The FIR runs once per vector for
  eight voices and is no longer the bottleneck.
- **Scalar engine.** All ten cases gain, by a small but consistent amount.
- **Lane worst case:** 1.618 M cycles (Prizma, BCS on).
- **Why K6 stays.** It does less work, it is more accurate, and it is
  nowhere slower beyond noise.
