# Explicit FMA in Mamut Analog, 2026-11-09

**Decision.** The operator allowed relaxing bit determinism against the
pre-FMA PCM, so that Mamut Analog can use fused multiply-add. The step was
planned in the 2026-11-08 SIMD discussion:
- explicit fusion, kernel by kernel;
- a tag before the switch;
- rebaselined signatures;
- a tolerance check against the old PCM;
- a measurement per kernel.

**Rule.** Nothing fuses implicitly:
- `-ffp-contract=off` stays.
- Mamut Analog fuses only where its source calls one of the helpers:
  - `ma_fmadd(a, b, c)` = a·b + c, and `ma_fnmadd(a, b, c)` = c − a·b, in
    `src/ma_voice_impl.h`;
  - `ml_fmadd`/`ml_fnmadd` in the lanes (`_mm256_fmadd_ps`/`_mm256_fnmadd_ps`).
- IEEE 754 specifies fma exactly: one rounding. Scalar code and the lanes
  therefore still agree bit for bit, and so do GCC and Clang.

**Tag.** `ma-pre-fma-20261109` (annotated, local) marks the last FMA-free
state, commit `0539402`.

## What changed

**Hardware requirement.**
- The core links no libm, so it requires hardware FMA.
- `ma_voice_impl.h` stops with `#error` unless the target has
  `__FMA__`, `__ARM_FEATURE_FMA` or `__FP_FAST_FMAF`.
- `__builtin_fmaf` becomes one `vfmadd`/`vfnmadd` instruction at `-O0`
  through `-O2`, with GCC 16.2.1 and with Clang 22.1.8.
- `check-core-symbols` stays clean: no `fmaf` reference.
- x86-64 already required x86-64-v3, which includes FMA. `ARCH_CFLAGS=`
  therefore no longer builds Mamut Analog (Makefile comment).
- The lane functions are `target("avx2,fma")`. `ma_lanes_supported` now also
  checks the FMA CPUID bit.

**Organ and EP.** Neither changes: `tw_sat` and `tw_sin_turns` keep their
FMA-free forms. Mamut Analog uses its own `ma_sat` and `ma_sin_turns`, with
the same polynomials and the same clamp, fused.

**Steps.** Each step is cumulative. Every step was gated by the
scalar/lane twins:
- `test_ma_bcs_lanes`, `test_ma_front_lanes`, `test_ma_raster_lanes`;
- the `test_ma_pool` engine twin;
- `test_ma_glas`, which includes the heavy Glas twin.

| Step | Kernel | Sites (scalar and lanes alike) |
|---|---|---|
| K0 | FIR zero taps | `decimate_oversample`/`ml_decimate31` sum only the 15 nonzero taps of the 31 (`OSC_HALFBAND_31_TAP`); `decimate_filter`/`ml_decimate7` only 5 of 7. Tap order is unchanged. Not fused. |
| K1 | FIR | Each tap is `out = fma(h, x, out)`. |
| K2 | Ladder, saturator | `ma_sat`/`ml_sat`: x·fma(x, x, 27) / fma(9x, x, 27). Ladder input: fnmadd(feedback, y4, gain·input). Stage: y = fma(d, g, s) with d = sat(u) − s; v = d·g is still computed for the state update. Mixer pressure, the warm sine and the BCS output use the same `ma_sat`. |
| K3 | BCS RK4 | The derivative's radius, radial, drive and the four component sums; `bcs_advance` as fma(scale, k, state); the final y + (h/6)·sum as fma; the output mix. |
| K4 | Oscillator | `smoothstep5`/`ml_smooth5` (BLEP and sync); `ma_sin_turns` as a fused Horner chain (warm sine, raster warp, grain sine); the wave mix in both previews and in `raw_wave_mix`; the triangle integrator and its sync restart; the raster warp; the grain sum. |
| K5 | Glas formant bank | The SVF high-pass numerator and denominator, bp, s1, lp, s2 and the bank sum. |

## K0 is exact; with FMA only the sign of zero can differ

**K0 alone.** Built with plain multiply and add, K0 passed the whole
`make test` with every pinned signature unchanged. The bench PCM hashes of
A and K0 are equal in all ten cases on both engines.

**Without FMA.** A zero tap adds 0·x. For finite x that is ±0, and adding ±0
never changes a sum: a non-fused sum of nonzero products never becomes −0.

**With FMA there is one exception.** A tiny negative product can make the
fused sum round to −0. A later zero tap, fma(0, x, −0) = +0, would have
turned that into +0. `test/ma_fir.c` now compares the 15-tap form against
the fused 31-tap traversal at every position:
- 62 results differ, and only in the sign of zero;
- every other bit is equal.

## Tolerance against the pre-FMA PCM

**The tool.** `driver/dump_ma_pcm.c` renders a fixed matrix into raw float32,
and `driver/pcm_diff.py` compares two such directories.
- The matrix: six factory patches × BCS off/on × Glas off/on × both engines,
  48 cases.
- Each case is 6 s at 48 kHz, 16 slots, unison 2: a four-note chord, a fifth
  note, mod wheel, pressure and a bend sweep, then a release at 4 s.
- `make dump-ma-pcm` writes the matrix to `build/ma-pcm`. The same driver
  source is built against both trees. Run:
  `build/ma3-fma-20261109/` (`pcm-A`, `pcm-K5`, `diff-A-K5.md`).

**Twins and rendering time.**
- In both A and K5, all 24 auto/scalar pairs are bit-identical.
- Whole matrix: 2 min 26 s for A, 2 min 14 s for K5.

**Difference K5 − A relative to the signal (RMS):**
- 46 of 48 cases lie between −94 and −136 dB, typically −100 to −113 dB.
- Prizma, BCS on, Glas off, is −95.5 dB.
- The worst is Lead, BCS on, Glas off: −82.9 dB, peak difference
  1.5·10⁻⁵ on a 0.018 peak.
- The 0–20 kHz figure is within 1 dB of the full-band figure.

**Why the difference grows in time.** It is largest with BCS and grows
during a held note. Per second, for Lead with BCS on:

| second | 0–1 | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4–5 (release) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| diff/signal dB | −102.4 | −97.3 | −95.9 | −77.8 | −70.9 |

- The BCS Hopf/Duffing pair is a free-running oscillator whose phase is
  neutrally stable. A changed rounding makes that phase drift slowly.
- The waveform difference therefore grows while the sound stays the same
  process.
- In the release the ratio rises further because the signal falls.
- Without BCS the difference stays near −105 dB until the release.

**Whole song.** Hurt industrial, about 4.5 min, rendered through the lanes:
`--rhythm ma3 --vocal-fat-all --gtr-fat`, the latest listening version.
- Stems differ from A by −96 dB (pads) to −111 dB (bass, rhythm).
- Third-octave band levels averaged over the song are equal to three
  decimals: the worst band moves by less than 0.0005 dB.

| Stem | diff/signal dB |
|---|---:|
| vocal | −103.3 |
| guitar | −103.7 |
| bass | −111.4 |
| pads | −96.1 |
| tonal | −101.6 |
| rhythm | −111.4 |
| mix | −104.2 |

New stem FNV-64 values, for example: rhythm `f36ce9fc29612262` (was
`de1f65c0459dc3d6`), mix `bf63c1ede2319e07` (was `f43e37cfd4efd520`).

## Speed

**Protocol.** Run `build/ma3-fma-20261109/` (`run-ab.sh`, `ab_table.py`,
`ab-summary.md`, `binary-sha256.txt`, `runs/`).
- Seven binaries (A, K0 … K5) from the same compiler (GCC 16.2.1).
- Ten patch/BCS cases, 8 slots held, wet, 128-frame blocks, 256 + 2,048
  blocks.
- PMU required, CPU 2 under `taskset`.
- Three epochs; the binary order rotates per epoch and reverses in the
  second.
- Both engines, 840 runs, 10:46–11:18.

**Host.** Quiet apart from a music player at about 3 % of one CPU.

**Stability.** The epoch spread is at most 3.2 % on the lanes and 6.5 % on
the scalar engine (Lead, BCS off); most cases are under 2 %. PCM hashes
are stable across epochs.

**Reading.**
- **Zero taps and the oscillator chains** give most of the gain.
- **The FIR's fused chain** is neutral. That fits the Haswell numbers: FMA
  latency 5 against add latency 3, on a latency-bound chain of 15 taps.
- **BCS** is neutral; its RK4 is a small part of the block.
- **K5 does not touch the bench**: the bench runs no Glas.
- **Lane worst case:** 1.630 M cycles (Prizma, BCS on), against 1.744 M for
  A.
- **No step is slower** beyond its noise, so every step stays. Fusion also
  rounds once instead of twice.

**Glas, in the Hurt industrial exhibit.**
- Method: `--vocal-fat-all --parts vocal --seconds 60`, CPU 2, A and K5
  alternating, counted with a small PMU wrapper (`cycles_run.c`; user
  cycles, inherited by threads).
- Instructions are deterministic: vocal 131.23 G → 126.71 G (−3.4 %); all
  parts, 60 s, 268.92 G → 256.04 G (−4.8 %).
- Cycles were noisy: `packagekitd` had started on CPU 2.
  - Vocal: A 86.8 and 78.3 G, K5 73.7 and 73.5 G, so K5 is at least 6 %
    faster.
  - All parts: A 166.9 and 177.8 G, K5 176.9 and 162.5 G, which does not
    resolve.
- An earlier back-to-back whole-song render (A first, then K5; 292 s
  against 309 s of user time) is not used: wall-clock time drifted with the
  host.

## Not done

- **Symmetric folding of the halfband pairs**, (x[i] + x[30−i])·h: about
  half the FIR multiplies again. It is a different rounding, so it would be
  a separate step.
- **Split accumulators in the FIR chain**, which would hide FMA latency.
- **Mixer, pressure, envelopes, and the scalar front and back ends** are not
  fused.
